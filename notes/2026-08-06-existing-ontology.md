# Existing Ontology — should I reuse them in my research?

## Which existing ontologies might be useful for my future research?

- **EMMO** — [Elementary Multiperspective Material Ontology](https://github.com/emmo-repo/EMMO): developed by the European Materials Modelling Council, it builds from applied physics and bottom-up scientific perspectives to represent materials modelling and characterization.
- **MatOnto** — [Material Ontology](https://ontolearner.readthedocs.io/benchmarking/materials_science_and_engineering/matonto.html): a domain ontology aligned with the Basic Formal Ontology (BFO), structured to capture material types, compositions, processing routes, and properties.
- **MDO** — [Materials Design Ontology](https://github.com/LiUSemWeb/Materials-Design-Ontology): designed primarily for quantum-mechanical and atomic-scale computational materials science, focusing on crystalline/periodic structures and molecular data.
- **MDS-Onto** — [Materials Data Science Ontology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-04938-5): a modular framework bridging specialized domains like photovoltaics and characterization methods to top-level BFO structures.
