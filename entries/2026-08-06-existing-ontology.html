Existing Ontology — should I reuse them in my research?

2026-08-06

Which existing ontologies might be useful for my future research?

  • EMMO — Elementary Multiperspective Material Ontology: developed by the European Materials Modelling Council, it builds from applied physics and bottom-up scientific perspectives to represent materials modelling and characterization.
  • MatOnto — Material Ontology: a domain ontology aligned with the Basic Formal Ontology (BFO), structured to capture material types, compositions, processing routes, and properties.
  • MDO — Materials Design Ontology: designed primarily for quantum-mechanical and atomic-scale computational materials science, focusing on crystalline/periodic structures and molecular data.
  • MDS-Onto — Materials Data Science Ontology: a modular framework bridging specialized domains like photovoltaics and characterization methods to top-level BFO structures.
